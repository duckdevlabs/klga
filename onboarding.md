KLGA helps you configure a realistic practice routine when you first use the app. The welcome screen introduces the main practice tools, and a two-step setup after sign-in saves your schedule, daily goal, and notification preferences.

## First Launch

The first time KLGA opens on a device, the **Welcome** screen introduces three parts of the app: practising with purpose, building consistency, and reviewing progress.

Tap **Continue** to proceed. KLGA remembers this choice on the device, so the welcome screen does not appear on later launches. If you are already signed in, Continue takes you to Home; otherwise it opens Login.

## Set Up Your Practice

After you sign in with an account that has not completed setup, KLGA opens **Set up your practice** over the Home screen. Existing accounts that already completed the current onboarding version skip this flow.

### Step 1: profile and schedule

1. Enter a **Display name** if you want one. It is optional and can contain up to 60 characters.
2. Enter a **Phone number** if needed. It is optional; when supplied, it must be a valid 7–20 character international-style number using digits and common separators.
3. Select any combination of **Practice days**. The counter updates as you select or clear days, and the chips follow your device's local first day of the week.
4. Tap **Daily goal** and choose between 5 and 240 minutes in 5-minute increments. Tap **Apply** to keep the selection or **Cancel** to leave it unchanged.
5. Tap **Next**. KLGA saves these details before moving to notifications.

You may continue without a display name, phone number, or selected practice day. If you close setup from this first step, it will be offered again because onboarding is not complete.

### Step 2: notifications

1. Tap **Enable notifications** and respond to the device permission request.
2. If permission was previously blocked, tap **Open notification settings**, change the permission in system settings, and return to KLGA. The setup screen refreshes its status when the app resumes.
3. Turn on **Practice reminders**, choose a time, and tap **Apply**. This option appears only when at least one practice day is selected; reminders are scheduled on those days.
4. Turn on **Streak protection** and choose an evening warning time. This alert is intended for days when you have an active streak but have not recorded practice.
5. Tap **Continue** to save the notification choices and finish setup.

The time picker follows your device's 12-hour or 24-hour preference and offers minutes in 5-minute increments. Notification options remain disabled until system permission is available.

## Resume or Retry Setup

- If you complete Step 1 and leave during Step 2, KLGA resumes at Step 2 the next time setup opens for that account.
- Use the back arrow on Step 2 to revise your profile, schedule, or daily goal without losing the current form values.
- If setup data cannot load, tap **Retry**.
- If saving fails, your selections remain available so you can try again.
- Selecting no practice days hides Practice Reminders. Streak Protection can still provide a daily at-risk alert.

## Change Preferences Later

Open **Settings** from the gear icon on Home to change practice days, reminder times, or streak protection after onboarding. Profile details can also be updated from the Profile editor.

Scheduled alerts use the device's local time. After travelling across time zones, launch KLGA so it can synchronize the schedule with the new local time.
