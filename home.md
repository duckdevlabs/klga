Home is the signed-in starting point for KLGA. Tap your **avatar** to open Profile or the **gear** to open Settings.

## Quick shortcuts

- **Metronome** opens the metronome tab.
- **Practice Calendar** shows a Monday-to-Sunday preview. Completed days use a check icon; tap the card to open Dashboard > Consistency.

## Academy cards

KLGA can display academy promotion cards below the fixed shortcuts. The app uses a cached valid version first when available, then refreshes it in the background. If there is no valid remote content, Home remains usable and simply omits the section.

Promotion cards can open approved in-app destinations or HTTPS links only.
